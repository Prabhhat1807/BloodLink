Workflow

How BloodLink Works

Designed for high-speed coordination during critical hospital and medical emergencies.

1
📋

1. Search or Request

Search for available blood donors by blood group and location, or submit a direct emergency request linked to a hospital.

2
⚙️

2. Smart Matching

Our algorithm checks biological compatibility using hash maps and calculates scores based on blood compatibility and location.

3
🚑

3. Instant Connection

Bubble sort ranks the top-matching donors at the top of the list, enabling immediate contact and rapid life-saving blood transfers.

Search & Match

Find Compatible Blood Donors

Search registered voluntary donors using biological compatibility rules and location proximity scoring.

💡
How Ranking Works: The backend calculates an intelligent match score: Exact Blood Match (+60 pts) or Compatible Match (+40 pts), plus Same City Proximity (+20 pts). Results are ordered by score using Bubble Sort ($O(n^2)$) in Python.
